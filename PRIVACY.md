# Privacy

Updated: 2026-11-21

Atemrunden creates a FIT activity on the Garmin watch using Garmin Connect IQ's
ActivityRecording API. The activity can include session timing and any standard
activity data that Garmin records for the device. It is saved on the watch and
may sync to the user's Garmin Connect account when the user syncs the watch.

The app does not send data to the project authors or to a project-operated
server. It has no account system, analytics, advertising, or third-party
tracking. Garmin handles data synced to Garmin Connect under its own
[privacy policy](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/privacy/connect/policy/).
